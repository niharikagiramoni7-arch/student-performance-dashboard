About The Platform

The Student Performance Dashboard provides universities, colleges, and schools with a unified system to record daily attendance, evaluate internal and external exam marks, track student submissions, dynamically calculate SGPA/CGPA with configurable credit point rubrics, and generate institutional compliance reports.

Core Academic Features

Everything students, faculty members, and academic administrators need in one single workflow.

📅

Attendance Tracking

Faculty can record student attendance per subject and date. Students view real-time percentage progress bars with warning alerts for attendance below 75%.

📊

Marks Management

Record internal assessments and semester exams with built-in validation limits. Automatic computation of total marks, grades (O, A+, A, B, C, F), and grade points.

📝

Assignment Tracking

Create coursework assignments with deadlines. Students can submit solutions directly and review evaluation scores and personalized faculty feedback.

⚡

CGPA Calculation

Automatic semester GPA and cumulative CGPA calculations based on actual academic scores and course credits. Updates dynamically upon marks entry.

📈

Performance Analytics

Interactive Chart.js visualizations including semester GPA trends, subject marks distributions, attendance breakdowns, and identified academic strengths and focus areas.

📑

Academic Reports

Institution-wide report generation for attendance, marks, CGPA merit rankings, and coursework submissions. Downloadable in CSV or formatted print-ready PDF.
